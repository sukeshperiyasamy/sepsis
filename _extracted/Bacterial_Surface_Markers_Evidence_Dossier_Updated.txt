Recommended diagnostic panel (near-term, evidence-backed):
1) LPS (endotoxin) assay — Gram-negative detection (LAL/MS confirmation). 2) Peptidoglycan fragment assay
(MDP/PGN LC-MS or ELISA) — pan-bacterial detection. 3) Capsular antigen tests — pathogen-specific rapid ID
(e.g., pneumococcal BinaxNOW). 4) LTA ELISA — Gram-positive support marker (S. aureus, S. pneumoniae). 5)
Optional: Flagellin or OMV proteomics for added sensitivity to motile pathogens or species-specific protein
signatures.
Pre-analytical & analytical considerations:
(cid:127) Blood matrix interference: LPS and other markers may be masked by serum proteins; sample prep and inhibitor removal are
crucial.
(cid:127) Timing: PGN fragments and soluble proteins can be transient — early sampling improves sensitivity.
(cid:127) Multiplexing: Combining broad (PGN) and specific (CPS, LPS) markers improves diagnostic accuracy.
(cid:127) Validation: Use culture-confirmed sepsis cohorts to validate sensitivity/specificity and clinical cutoffs.
