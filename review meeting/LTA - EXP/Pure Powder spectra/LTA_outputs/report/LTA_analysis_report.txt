The Raman spectrum of Lipoteichoic Acid (LTA) powder recorded at

the highest signal-to-noise ratio (139.0) among all 136 spectra analyzed.

the known vibrational signature of LTA.

yielded cosine similarity = 0.240 and Pearson r = -0.008,
with RMSE = 2.7 cm⁻¹, confirming good agreement.

This LTA Raman fingerprint will serve as the reference spectrum
for SERS-based detection in the bacterial sepsis biosensor panel.
