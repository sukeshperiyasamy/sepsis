Paper-Ready Description

Raman measurements were performed using a BWTEK i-Raman Plus (Model: BWS465-785H) equipped with a 785 nm excitation laser (maximum output 495 mW) and a BAC102-785E Raman microscope using 20× and 50× objectives. The SERS substrates were fabricated on 2-inch, single-crystal, n-type phosphorus-doped silicon wafers with ⟨100⟩ crystal orientation, 1–10 Ω·cm resistivity, 280 ± 25 µm thickness, and a single-side polished surface, grown by the Czochralski (CZ) method. Silver was deposited onto the polished silicon surface to generate plasmonic hotspots for SERS measurements.
