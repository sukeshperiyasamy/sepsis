DFT and MD Simulations of Saccharides (GlcNAc) For monosaccharides like 2-acetamido-2-deoxy-d-glucose (GlcNAc), Raman and Raman Optical Activity (ROA) spectra are simulated using a hybrid QM/MM approach to capture structural dynamics and solvation:
Conformational Averaging: Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations are first used to generate a representative ensemble of structures at ambient conditions (300 K)
. Snapshots are uniformly extracted from the trajectory to account for the molecule's conformational flexibility (such as α/β anomeric equilibria and ring puckering)
.
Solvent Effects: A micro-solvation environment is maintained by keeping the central saccharide and explicit water molecules within a 3 Å cutoff
. The system is modeled using the ONIOM method, where the explicit water molecules are treated at the Molecular Mechanics (MM) level and the saccharide at the Quantum Mechanics (QM) level
. Long-range polarization from the bulk solvent is modeled using the COSMO implicit solvation model
.
DFT Calculations: The solute is calculated at the B3LYP/6-311++G** level of theory
. Unrestrained 10-step geometry optimization is performed before calculating vibrational frequencies and Raman intensities under a harmonic approximation
.
DFT-MD Simulations of Alanine Dipeptide in Liquid Water To understand the vibrational signatures of alanine backbones (specifically the Ac-Ala-NHMe analog, or "2-Ala"), researchers employ finite-temperature Car–Parrinello MD (CPMD) simulations:
Solvent Effects and Structural Dynamics: The 2-Ala peptide is immersed in a periodic box with 118 explicit water molecules and simulated using the DFT/BLYP electronic representation
. This explicitly captures the strong hydrogen bonding between the liquid water and the peptide, revealing a highly zwitterionic character of the peptide bonds in solution
.
Conformational Averaging: Rather than analyzing a single static structure, the MD trajectory captures the dynamic equilibrium of the peptide transitioning between polyproline II (PII), unfolded β-sheet, and folded right-handed α-helix (α 
R
​
 ) conformations
.
Spectra Generation: The infrared (IR) spectrum is generated directly from the Fourier Transform of the dipole moment time correlation function, taking into account vibrational anharmonicities
. To isolate the solute's specific spectrum from the bulk water, the total electronic dipole moment is decomposed using Maximally Localized Wannier Functions

Classical Machine Learning: Algorithms such as Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest (RF), k-Nearest Neighbors (k-NN), and Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) can achieve greater than 99% accuracy in classifying these endotoxins after proper baseline correction

Deep Learning: A specialized convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture named RamanNet has been developed specifically for Raman spectral analysis. Unlike standard CNNs that use translational equivalence, RamanNet uses shifted densely connected blocks to maintain context localization for specific Raman shifts
