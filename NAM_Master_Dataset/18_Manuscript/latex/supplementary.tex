\section*{S1. Computational details}

Geometry optimisation and harmonic vibrational frequency calculations were
performed with Gaussian~16 using the route section

\begin{center}
\texttt{\# opt=(calcfc,tight) freq=raman b3lyp/6-311++g(d,p)}\\
\texttt{empiricaldispersion=gd3bj geom=connectivity int=ultrafine scf=(tight,xqc)}
\end{center}

\noindent
on the $\beta$ anomer of N-acetylmuramic acid taken from the PubChem 3D
conformer record (CID~5462244). The optimisation converged to a stationary point and the calculation reached normal termination.
The optimised structure comprises 39 atoms (C$_{11}$H$_{19}$NO$_8$) and gave
111 normal modes, none imaginary. Harmonic wavenumbers were scaled by 0.980
below 1800~\wn{} and 0.967 above.

\section*{S2. Experimental dataset}

98 independent Raman spectra of N-acetylmuramic acid powder were acquired on a
B\&W Tek BWS465-785H system (784.92~nm, 2048-pixel detector, two detector
readings averaged per spectrum) across five integration times (5, 10, 15, 20,
25~s) and sixteen laser power levels (5--80\% in 5\% steps). No detector
saturation occurred at any condition. The 40 spectra with SNR $\geq 100$ were
used to construct the reference spectrum reported in the main text; their mean
pairwise correlation coefficient is 0.957.
